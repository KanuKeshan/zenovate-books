Bank reconciliation
Monthly reconciliation
The card above nets your entire history together, so two offsetting gaps in different months (e.g. a transaction recorded a month late) can silently cancel out and still show "Reconciled" overall. This breaks it out month by month so a gap can't hide.
Fingerprint count mismatches
The most reliable duplicate/missing-transaction check: every bank row and every recorded Expense/Invoice is grouped by its underlying date+amount+description fingerprint, and this lists any fingerprint where the two counts don't match — regardless of what the Match column says. Start here if you suspect duplicates or missing imports. If the list below is long, skip the row-by-row cleanup and use "Rebuild from bank statements" instead — it wipes every bank-derived invoice/expense and regenerates exactly one per categorized bank row, guaranteeing the counts tie out. It only touches records that came from a bank import (manually-entered invoices/expenses are left alone), and it's safe to click more than once — it always rebuilds from scratch rather than adding on top, so it can't create new duplicates.
Date mismatches in matched records
Every row below shows as "matched" in the table below and isn't a reconciliation gap by itself — but its recorded date and its actual bank-clearing date disagree, which is exactly what can make two different months each look off even though nothing is "unmatched." Most common cause: an invoice/expense entered by hand before the matching bank statement was imported. If you only ever import from bank statements (no manual entries), this table should always be empty.
